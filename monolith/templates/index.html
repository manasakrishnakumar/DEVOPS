About This System

The Bangalore Climate Analytics System is a comprehensive climate monitoring and prediction platform designed to analyze historical climate patterns, detect anomalies, and forecast future climate conditions for Bangalore city.

Mathematical Framework:
  • Z-Score: Z = (X - μ) / σ - Standardized anomaly detection
  • Climate Extreme Score (CES): CES = 0.6×Z_rain + 0.4×Z_temp (humidity shown for reference only, not used in CES)
  • Anomaly Classification: |Z| ≥ 1.300 (Strong), 1.051 ≤ |Z| < 1.300 (Moderate), else Normal (WMO SPI percentiles)
  • Forecasting: Seasonal ARIMA models with 95% confidence intervals
